Starting point

Slow approvals and no developer time

A trading platform. Approving and publishing content was very complex, because every change had to be coordinated with the client’s lawyers, security team and PR department. Recommendations that required a developer were practically never implemented. Link budget: $1,000 a month.

The key move

Automation built by the SEO specialist, not by developers

Since anything that needed a developer stalled, the SEO specialist created regularly updated content and built the automation of its updates in n8n, a workflow automation tool, without using any of the client’s developer resources.

Result

From 48,000 to 200,000+ visits a month

Over the long run, total search traffic from Google grew from 48,000 to 200,000+ visits a month. After the SEO specialist built the content-update automation, non-brand traffic grew from 6,200 to 38,900 visits a month, and then by another 85%, or +232,000 visits in six months.

Result 3 · German version

8× in a year, then +181,000 visits in six months

After the automation, non-brand search traffic of the German version grew 8× in a year, from 3,490 to 28,100 visits a month. The average position improved from 19.6 to 8.3. The growth then continued: over the next six months it added 181,000 visits.

Result 4 · French version

4× in a year, then +51,300 visits in six months

After the content-update automation, non-brand search traffic of the French version grew 4× in a year, from 2,700 to 10,800 visits a month. The average position improved from 19.7 to 10.3. Over the next six months the growth added another 51,300 visits.
