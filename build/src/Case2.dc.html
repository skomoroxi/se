SEO and brand protection for a SaaS project

The company lost its established domain and brand name. The new brand recaptured most of the old brand’s traffic and grew non-brand traffic to 15,000+ visits a month.

Starting point

A lost domain, and no way to link old and new

A business in the SMS verification niche lost its established domain and brand name. Showing any direct connection between the old and the new brand was prohibited, including redirects, canonical or hreflang tags between the old and the new domain.

Result

70%+ of the old brand’s traffic, and DR from 0 to 56

A strategy covering both the new site’s content and the information environment on external resources brought the new site more than 70% of the old brand’s traffic, and AI assistants now agree that the new brand replaces the lost one. A large part of the old site’s backlink profile moved to the new site, so its DR grew from 0 to 56.

Result 1 · Brand traffic

1.43 million Google clicks for the new brand

Search Console, end of December 2025 to October 2026: 1.43M clicks, 4.27M impressions, 33.6% CTR and an average position of 6.6. Daily clicks rose from zero to 4,000–8,000.

Result 2 · Non-brand traffic

Non-brand traffic grew to 15,000+ visits a month

At the start we launched an active link-building and PR campaign to capture the old site’s brand traffic: a network of 30+ sites and a large number of publications. This brought link equity naturally and formed a consensus among AI assistants about the connection between the old and the new brand. We also developed the minimum set of pages needed to launch language versions of the project.
