Tripling traffic for file conversion software

In 1 year and 9 months, traffic grew three times, from 187,000 to 564,000 visits a month, thanks to work on links and language versions of the site.

Starting point

A file conversion business with seasonal demand

A business in the file conversion software niche. Demand is seasonal.

What we did

Links, programmatic SEO and 14 languages

We worked on links and content (programmatic SEO plus targeted optimization), mainly for pages about file formats the client’s software can convert. The number of languages the site is translated into grew from 6 to 14.

Result

From 187,000 to 564,000 visits a month

Search traffic from Google grew three times. The screenshots below show 2+ years of traffic and the growth trend.
